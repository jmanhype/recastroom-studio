Privacy, terms, and rights

Local demo privacy

The default local deployment creates no account and uses no advertising trackers. In preview and local ComfyUI modes, uploads stay in your configured local data directory; hosted Comfy mode sends the prepared workflow and media to the deployment you configure. Clearing browser state does not delete server job files.
