Why recasting is hard

A replacement can lose the take

One replacement can change three things

A new face can move the camera, stretch the timing, or invent speech that was never in the source.

RecastRoom keeps those layers separate so a review can tell whether the performance survived.

Identity is only one layer

RecastRoom keeps identity, performance, scene, and audio as separate workflow inputs instead of blending them into one hidden prompt.

The edit survives replacement

Advanced project tools record source cuts, take selections, and export checks so review is repeatable.

Permissions

Source and likeness permissions

RecastRoom asks you to confirm source ownership, reference permission, and consent for every identifiable person before preparation. The gate is part of the workflow, not a footer warning.

The application cannot prove legal clearance for you. It records your permission confirmation and keeps the production path closed until that declaration exists.

FAQ

Questions before you run it

Does RecastRoom recreate the voice?

No. The source audio is conditioned into the workflow and restored into the final review output. RecastRoom does not synthesize a replacement voice.

Can I use any footage?

No. Use a clip you own, license, or have explicit permission to modify. Public domain and openly licensed archives can work when their terms allow modification.

What counts as an authorized reference?

An image or description representing a replacement likeness that you have permission to use for that purpose, together with consent from every identifiable person where required.

What happens if you cannot confirm permission?

The workspace blocks preparation until you record permission. A custom workflow cannot bypass that app gate, and every app mode rechecks the recorded job summary before a run.

Does the output preserve exact audio?

The review output restores the original source audio. An edited export follows the selected audio policy. Final published work still needs your own review.

Can I bring my own Comfy workflow?

Yes. A compatible custom workflow can be imported, but it must use the expected RecastRoom inputs for routing, audio restoration, permission, and review.

Where is job history recorded?

Prepared job summaries, workflow JSON, prompts, result videos, paired reviews, and status records remain with each job.

What should I check before publishing?

Watch the full result with audio, compare synchronized frames, verify rights records, and confirm that the final media meets the platform and license requirements for your release.
