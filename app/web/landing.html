Permission before production

Source and likeness permissions

RecastRoom asks you to confirm source ownership, reference permission, and consent for every identifiable person before preparation. The gate is part of the workflow, not a footer warning.

The application cannot prove legal clearance for you. It records your operator attestation and keeps the production path closed until that declaration exists.

Answers

Questions before you run it

Does RecastRoom recreate the voice?

No. The source audio is conditioned into the workflow and restored into the final review output. Drama 3 narration is used for the project explainer, not for changing the performer’s voice.

Can I use any footage?

No. Use a clip you own, license, or have explicit permission to modify. Public domain and openly licensed archives can work when their terms allow modification.

What counts as an authorized reference?

An image or description representing a replacement likeness that you have permission to use for that purpose, together with consent from every identifiable person where required.

What happens if you cannot make the attestation?

The workspace blocks preparation until you record the operator attestation. A custom workflow cannot bypass that app gate, and all backend modes recheck the recorded manifest before execution.

Does the output preserve exact audio?

The archived proof records an unchanged source stream copy. An edited export follows its codec-aware edit map and validates the selected audio policy. Final published work still needs your own review.

Can I bring my own Comfy graph?

Yes. A compatible custom graph can be imported, but it must retain the routing, audio lock, operator attestation, and review contracts enforced by RecastRoom.

Where is provenance recorded?

Prepared manifests, workflow JSON, media probes, review artifacts, and hashes remain with each job and release package.

What should I check before publishing?

Watch the full result with audio, compare synchronized frames, verify rights records, and confirm that the final media meets the platform and license requirements for your release.
