# Operator attestation and responsibility policy

RecastRoom Studio is designed for source clips and reference likenesses that you own, license, or have explicit permission to modify.

## Required before generation

1. Source authority
   - you created the clip,
   - you have a license permitting modification, or
   - you have written permission from the rights holder.

2. Performer/reference consent
   - the replacement performer has consented,
   - every identifiable person in the source has consented where required,
   - consent is especially important for commercial, comedic, sexual, political, or deceptive contexts.

3. Model and workflow licenses
   - obey the MiniMax H3 model license,
   - obey the licenses of all LoRAs and custom nodes,
   - keep the RecastRoom Studio MIT license notice when you fork or redistribute the app.

## Prohibited uses

Do not use RecastRoom Studio to create:

- unlicensed celebrity or public-figure likenesses,
- sexual or degrading depictions of real people,
- political misinformation or fake statements,
- fraudulent evidence,
- recreations of deceased performers without appropriate authorization,
- unlicensed commercial media redistribution.

The operator attestation is a user responsibility. RecastRoom Studio does not inspect media provenance, verify consent, validate model licensing, or prove legal clearance. Archive metadata is discovery evidence, not clearance.
